Features

Every jump gets a way back.

You skip ahead to find a part, miss it, and lose your place. This brings the place back.

Back to the exact second

Click the timeline, press a number key, or tap an arrow. Whatever moved you, Ctrl+Z puts you at the second you left, and a small message shows the time.

Ctrl+Z

Drags count once

Scrub across the whole video and one Ctrl+Z returns to where you started.

Redo included

Went back too far? Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y takes you forward again.

Ctrl+Y

Stays out of the way

It only runs on YouTube, asks for no permissions and sends nothing anywhere. In the search box and comments, Ctrl+Z still undoes your typing like normal. Up to 100 steps are remembered per video.

Install

Four steps, about a minute.

It isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so you load it yourself once. After that it just works.

  1. Unzip the download

    Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Keep the folder somewhere it won't get deleted.

    yt-undo-seek

  2. Open extensions

    Paste this in a new tab, then turn on Developer mode in the top right.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked and choose the yt-undo-seek folder.

  4. Try it on YouTube

    Refresh any YouTube tab, click the timeline, then press Ctrl+Z.

FAQ

Questions.

Why do I need Developer mode?

The extension isn't published in the Chrome Web Store, so Chrome only loads it from a folder. Developer mode allows that. Nothing else changes in your browser.

Does it collect any data?

No. It asks for no permissions, makes no network requests and stores nothing. It only watches the video's playback position while a YouTube tab is open.

Does the history carry over to the next video?

No. Opening another video starts a fresh history. Seeks during ads are ignored.

The download button did nothing. What now?
